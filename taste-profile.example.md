## 2. Hard Negative Filters (`-100` Auto-Drop)

- **Shorts & micro-clips:** Drop any upload under 4 minutes (`240s`).
- **Music, ambient, or gaming:** Drop `lofi`, `study music`, `mix`, `gameplay`, `walkthrough`, `24/7`.
- **Generic hype without a demo:** Drop vague speculation that does not show a real system, codebase, or benchmark on screen.
